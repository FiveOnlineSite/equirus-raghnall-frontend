INCIDENT REPORTING GUIDE

1. Make the location safe and arrange emergency assistance where needed.
2. Record the date, time, location, people involved, and an initial description.
3. Photograph the scene and preserve relevant documents or damaged items.
4. Take reasonable steps to limit further loss, retaining receipts and records.
5. Notify your claims adviser and insurer promptly under the policy requirements.
6. Keep a log of actions, costs, conversations, and correspondence.
