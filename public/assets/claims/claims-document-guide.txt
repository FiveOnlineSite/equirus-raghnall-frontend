CLAIMS DOCUMENT GUIDE

Documents vary by claim and policy. Start with:
- Policy schedule and relevant wording
- Date, time, location, and description of the incident
- Photographs and available inspection reports
- Asset ownership, purchase, maintenance, and valuation records
- Repair estimates and invoices
- Financial records relevant to interrupted operations
- Correspondence with the insurer, assessor, and other parties

Keep originals and speak with your claims adviser about the records needed for your claim.
